# Calendly alternatives

Investigate replacing Calendly with a scheduling tool that:

- Shows available times while marking some as preferred.
- Optionally lets the recipient compare those times with availability in their own calendar.
- Supports event-specific durations, date-specific hours, time-zone conversion, booking windows, calendar conflict checks, video links, and shareable booking pages.

The primary requirement is preferred times. Recipient-calendar visibility is secondary.

## Questions

- Can an existing product distinguish preferred, acceptable, and unavailable times on one booking page?
- Can it show the recipient's conflicts without exposing their event details?
- If no product does both well, what is the smallest Calendly replacement or booking-page layer worth building?

## Candidates

- Cal.com
- SavvyCal
- Zencal
- Cronofy or Nylas for calendar availability APIs
